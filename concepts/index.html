CAE Engineering Hub

Interface explorations

Six ways to launch the same seven tools. These pages are prototypes. The production launcher is separate.

  1. Concept A · Split-pane tool explorer

    A grouped tool list sits beside an inspector. Choosing a tool shows its preview, description, tags, and status here, then opens the tool in its own workspace.

    Open concept

  2. Concept B · Compact tile workspace

    The bench is the page. Tools are modular tiles of different sizes, with category and search as a thin working bar and no introductory hero.

    Open concept

  3. Concept C · Command center

    Search, Ctrl or Command K, recently used tools, favorites, and category commands are the working surface for repeated daily use.

    Open concept

  4. Concept D · Navigation rail and canvas

    A narrow icon rail leaves a wide canvas of category lanes. Category names stay available to keyboard and pointer users, and can be pinned open.

    Open concept

  5. Concept E · Tool index

    An engineering directory pairs each name and purpose with category, status, a compact preview, and a direct open action, in a row that can grow past the current seven tools.

    Open concept

  6. Concept F · Work route

    Tools are stations on a CAE job path. Selecting a station expands it in place, from references and materials through calculations, the pre-run check, standards, and weight data.

    Open concept
